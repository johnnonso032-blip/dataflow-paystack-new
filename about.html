About Us

QuickData is an online platform that helps people in Nigeria buy airtime and mobile data. It also supports resellers, such as POS operators, who want to buy these services at reseller prices and sell them to their own customers.

What you can do on QuickData

  • Create an account with your name, phone number and email address.
  • Fund a QuickData wallet and use it to buy airtime and data.
  • Buy airtime and data for the networks and plans shown in the app. Supported networks and plans can change.
  • Apply for reseller access if you want to sell airtime and data to your own customers.
  • See your recent transactions and wallet activity in your account.

How it works

  • Payments are made through Paystack, a third-party payment provider. QuickData does not see or store your full card details.
  • Airtime and data orders are delivered through a third-party supplier. Delivery depends on that supplier and on the mobile network.
  • QuickData is not a bank or a financial institution. Your wallet is only for buying services on QuickData. It is not a bank account or a savings account.

Where we operate

QuickData serves customers in Nigeria.
