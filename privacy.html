Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal information QuickData collects, why we need it, how we use and protect it, and how you can contact us about it. It is written with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 in mind.

1. Who is responsible for your information

QuickData, operating in Nigeria. Contact: standardbank014762@gmail.com or WhatsApp +234 708 823 8970.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number and email address, and your password. Your password is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form, so we cannot read it.
  • Optional profile photo, if you choose to add one.
  • Reseller details: a business or shop name, if you apply to become a reseller.
  • Transaction records: orders (network, plan or amount, recipient phone number, time and status), wallet funding, wallet debits and refunds, and payment references.
  • Information stored on your device: your browser keeps a login token and a copy of your recent orders so the app can work smoothly.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address when you use the website.

Card details are entered on Paystack’s secure checkout. QuickData does not see or store your full card details.

3. Why we need it

  • To create and manage your account and keep it secure.
  • To process payments, fund your wallet and deliver airtime and data.
  • To show your transaction history and handle failed orders, refunds and complaints.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse.
  • To answer your questions and keep records we are required to keep.

4. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to run the service:

  • Paystack processes payments and receives the payment details and your email address.
  • Our airtime and data supplier (VTpass) receives the recipient phone number, network and amount or plan so it can deliver the order.
  • Hosting and database providers (Vercel and Upstash) store and process the data that makes the app work.
  • We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect against fraud.

Some of these providers may store or process data outside Nigeria. We do not sell your personal information.

5. How we protect it

  • Passwords are stored in hashed form.
  • The website is served over HTTPS.
  • Each user can only see their own account information, and administrative functions need a separate admin password.
  • We limit repeated login attempts.

No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password private.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account and transaction records while your account is active, and for as long as we need them to handle disputes, keep accounting records and meet legal duties.

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may have rights over your personal data, including the right to ask what we hold about you, to have wrong information corrected, to ask for information to be deleted where we are not required to keep it, to object to or restrict some uses, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To use any of these rights, contact us. We may need to confirm who you are first. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

8. Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser’s storage to keep you signed in and to show your recent orders. We do not use advertising trackers. Paystack’s checkout may use its own cookies or storage. Please see Paystack’s own privacy policy.

9. Children

QuickData is for adults and is not directed at children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
