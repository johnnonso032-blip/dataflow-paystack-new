Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how to report a failed or incomplete airtime or data order, and how we review it.

1. Successful, pending and failed orders

  • Successful: the supplier confirmed that the airtime or data was delivered to the phone number you entered. The app shows success (or delivered). A successful order is final and is not refundable.
  • Pending: the order is still being processed. A pending order is not a failed order. Please wait and check the status again in your transaction history. We do not refund a pending order until the final result is known.
  • Failed: the supplier reported that it could not deliver the order. The app shows failed.

2. Failed orders paid from your wallet

If a wallet order fails because the supplier confirms the failure, the amount is returned to your wallet automatically. The refund appears in your wallet activity. We do not give a processing time, because it depends on when the supplier confirms the result.

3. Failed orders paid directly with Paystack

If you paid for an order directly with Paystack and it failed or did not arrive, the refund is not automatic. Please report it to us. We will review the payment and supplier records. If the review confirms that the order failed, we will arrange a refund or redeliver the order. We will tell you which one and when.

4. Wallet funding problems

If Paystack charged you but your wallet was not credited, please contact us with your Paystack payment reference and a screenshot of your receipt. We will check the payment.

5. What is not refundable

  • Orders that were delivered successfully.
  • Orders sent to a wrong phone number or the wrong network that you entered or chose yourself.
  • Change of mind after delivery.

6. How to report a problem

Contact us by email at standardbank014762@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +234 708 823 8970. Please send the phone number that received the order, the network, the amount or plan, the date and time, the order or payment reference, and a screenshot if you have one. See our Contact Us page.

7. Other options

You can also raise a concern with Paystack or with your bank about a payment.
